⚠ Tips to identify phishing messages
✔ Treat unexpected sign-in alerts carefully. An unfamiliar location, device or sign-in notification can create urgency. Pause and verify before taking action.
✔ Do not trust the link in the alert. Attackers can imitate Microsoft 365 and security notifications. Do not use an unexpected email link to secure or unlock an account.
✔ Verify through a trusted route. Open your approved Microsoft 365 or identity portal directly from a known bookmark or use your normal corporate access route.
✔ Check the destination. Before opening a link, inspect the destination and confirm that it belongs to the expected organization or service.
✔ Never provide credentials from an unexpected prompt. If a message asks you to sign in, enter a password or approve an unexpected request, stop and verify it independently.
✔ Report the message. Use the organization's approved Outlook reporting process or contact the Information Security team so suspicious activity can be investigated.
