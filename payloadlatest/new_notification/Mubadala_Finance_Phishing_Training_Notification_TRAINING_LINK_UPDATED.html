⚠ Tips to identify phishing messages
✔ Check the sender and domain. Confirm that the sender's address and domain are exactly what you expect. Lookalike domains and small spelling changes are common phishing indicators.
✔ Pause when money is involved. Unexpected requests involving payments, reimbursements, invoices or banking information deserve extra verification before you act.
✔ Verify independently. Use a known phone number, trusted contact or approved internal process to confirm financial requests rather than replying to the message.
✔ Inspect links before opening them. Hover over links and check the real destination. Do not trust a familiar-looking display name or button alone.
✔ Do not disclose credentials. Never enter Microsoft 365 or corporate credentials after following an unexpected email link. Open approved services directly.
✔ Report suspicious messages. Use the organization's approved Outlook reporting process or contact the Information Security team when something does not look right.
